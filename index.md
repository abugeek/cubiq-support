# CUBIQ — Privacy Policy and Support

Last updated: 14 November 2026

## Privacy

CUBIQ is an offline cube solver and coach for iPhone and iPad. CUBIQ collects no personal data. It has no accounts, analytics, advertising, tracking, or server uploads. The bundled cube.js solver runs on your device.

### Camera

When you scan a cube, the camera reads its colors on your device. Camera frames are processed in memory and are not stored or uploaded in release builds. You can enter colors manually instead.

### Data on your device

Settings and progress, including solves, lessons, practice and timer results, are stored locally. You can reset progress in CUBIQ settings. Deleting the app removes its local data. Your device's backup settings may include app data in your personal backup.

### Children

CUBIQ does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

### Changes and contact

If the app's privacy practices change, this policy will be updated. For privacy questions, contact [aabdupattaev@icloud.com](mailto:aabdupattaev@icloud.com).

## Support

- **Solve a cube:** choose Solve my cube and scan each side, or enter the colors manually. CUBIQ then guides you one turn at a time.
- **Learn:** choose Learn to solve for lessons and practice.
- **Camera trouble:** use bright, even lighting and avoid glare, or enter the colors manually.
- **First solve:** preparing the offline solver may take about 20 seconds; later solves are faster.
- **Language:** change it in CUBIQ settings or iOS Settings.
- **Delete progress:** choose Reset progress in CUBIQ settings.
- **Color symbols:** turn them on in CUBIQ settings if colors are hard to distinguish.

For help or to report a problem, email [aabdupattaev@icloud.com](mailto:aabdupattaev@icloud.com).
